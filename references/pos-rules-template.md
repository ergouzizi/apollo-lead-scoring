# POS Rules Template

Use this file when the user adds POS machines as a new product direction. Treat it as a starting point until salesperson feedback calibrates it.

## Product Boundary To Confirm

Ask or infer what POS product is being sold:

- Android POS terminal.
- Desktop cashier POS.
- Handheld POS.
- Payment terminal.
- POS all-in-one with printer/customer display/scanner.
- Restaurant/retail self-service kiosk.

Clarify whether the company sells only hardware or also software. Do not assume POS follows digital signage's CMS rule; POS may be hardware, software, payment, or both.

## Likely High Priority

- POS system vendors with hardware deployment.
- Restaurant/retail SaaS that supplies or integrates POS hardware.
- POS hardware distributors, resellers, or service providers.
- Retail/restaurant system integrators.
- Payment service providers only when they deploy POS terminals or merchant hardware.
- Hospitality/retail solution providers with cashier hardware evidence.

## Likely Medium Priority

- Store management or restaurant software vendors with plausible POS hardware compatibility but no supply evidence.
- ERP/retail management systems with cashier workflows.
- IT integrators serving retail/hospitality with project delivery evidence.

## Likely Low Or Unsuitable

- Ordinary restaurants, stores, hotels, cafes, supermarkets as terminal users.
- Pure payment gateway with no terminal hardware.
- Pure ecommerce, loyalty, CRM, marketing, or delivery platforms.
- Pure accounting software.
- Hardware unrelated to POS checkout.

## Suggested Apollo Recall

Industries:

- computer software
- information technology & services
- retail
- restaurants
- hospitality
- business supplies & equipment
- financial services only for payment-terminal batches

Keywords:

- POS system
- retail POS
- restaurant POS
- cashier system
- checkout system
- POS hardware
- payment terminal
- merchant terminal
- point of sale

Avoid using scanner, RFID, receipt printer, and cash drawer as primary POS keywords unless the product catalog includes those items.

## Judgement Fields

Product direction examples:

- 安卓POS终端
- 桌面收银POS机
- 餐饮/零售POS硬件
- 支付/商户终端
- 暂不适合

Cut-in examples:

- 询问其 POS/门店系统是否需要兼容第三方安卓收银终端。
- 询问其餐饮客户部署时是否需要稳定供货的收银硬件。
- 先确认其是否只是支付网关；若无终端硬件部署，不建议优先开发。
