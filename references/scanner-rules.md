# Scanner Rules

## Product Boundary

Scanner products are plug-and-play hardware peripherals: barcode scanner, barcode reader, Auto-ID, AIDC, POS scanner, and industrial code-reading hardware.

Do not require customers to develop their own software. High-priority scanner customers are companies that sell, supply, distribute, select, integrate, recommend, or deploy scanner/Auto-ID hardware for their customers.

## High Priority

- Auto-ID channels.
- Barcode scanner / barcode reader distributors or resellers.
- Scanner system integrators.
- WMS / POS / MES / traceability / smart factory solution providers with evidence of scanner peripheral selection, implementation, supply, or compatibility.
- Industrial automation / fixed-mount reader / machine vision integrators with barcode identification or traceability hardware evidence.

## Medium Priority

- POS/WMS/MES/software companies with strong workflow need but no explicit scanner hardware page.
- ERP/Odoo/warehouse/retail systems where a scanner compatibility discussion is plausible.

## Low Or Unsuitable

- Pure warehouse, logistics, 3PL, cold chain, end retail stores, ordinary factories.
- Freight forwarding, customs clearance, transportation.
- Pure software/payment/ecommerce/marketing with no scanner hardware, peripheral compatibility, or integration evidence.
- Ordinary manufacturing/trading/repair.
- Competitors or same-category manufacturers unless the user wants competitor intelligence.

## Deleted Or Dangerous Broad Keywords

Do not expand scanner into these directions unless the user explicitly says so:

- handheld terminal
- mobile computer
- PDA
- RFID
- RFID warehouse
- label printer
- receipt printer
- fulfillment
- 3PL
- order picking
- cold chain
- shop floor data collection

## Scoring Levels

- 高 `80-100`: direct scanner/Auto-ID sales, distribution, integration, or deployment evidence.
- 中 `60-79`: strong WMS/POS/MES/automation scenario but incomplete scanner hardware evidence.
- 低 `40-59`: adjacent software or operations scenario; scanner opportunity is only exploratory.
- 不适合 `<40`: terminal user, logistics/transport, generic IT, unrelated company, or evidence mismatch.
