# Digital Signage Rules

## Product Boundary

The company provides only small indoor digital signage hardware terminals. The practical product sizes are `10.1 / 14 / 15.6 inches`.

The company does not provide customer-side CMS, content publishing, screen management, or ad delivery software. High-value customers must already have software/platform/project delivery capability or be able to integrate/supply the hardware into their own solution.

## High Priority

- Smart display software companies.
- Digital signage CMS / SaaS / content publishing / screen management platforms.
- Digital signage platforms that can deploy to hardware terminals.
- Menu screen systems, in-store content publishing systems, retail media networks with own CMS/screen management.
- Indoor small-screen hardware channels, AV/system integrators, suppliers, or solution providers that can deliver small indoor screen projects.
- Advertising / retail media / in-store media companies only when they have own CMS, screen network, or project delivery and need third-party small digital signage hardware.

## Target Scenarios

- Shelf screens.
- Counter screens.
- Tabletop menu screens.
- Restaurant/cafe menu screens.
- Hotel front desk screens.
- Beauty/retail display screens.
- Supermarket/fashion store small screens.
- Meeting room signage.
- Electronic door signs.
- Smart office small screens.

## Exclusions Or Downgrades

- Outdoor DOOH/OOH, outdoor LED, bus/taxi/highway outdoor media.
- Large commercial displays, LED walls, video walls, splicing walls, mall atrium screens, exhibition hall big-screen projects.
- Pure creative advertising, media buying, media sales, online ad platforms with no CMS/screen network/hardware demand.
- Banking/financial IT, enterprise service management, branch IT support, queue management, ticketing, service evaluation terminals.
- Office furniture, space optimization, fixtures, interior design, fit-out.
- Generic IT services, cybersecurity, software outsourcing, digital transformation with no digital signage CMS or hardware evidence.
- POS/payment/cashier systems with no digital signage CMS/menu screen/small display evidence.

## Critical Lessons

- Do not treat a company as high score because Apollo Keywords or search query text contains `digital signage`.
- Do not interpret bare `ESL` as Electronic Shelf Label. In education batches it often means English as a Second Language.
- Treat exclusion words by main business, not by one occurrence. If a company has real digital signage/CMS/hardware evidence, downgrade thoughtfully instead of zeroing it out.

## Scoring Levels

- 高 `80-100`: clear CMS/software/screen management, indoor small-screen signage solution, or strong indoor signage hardware/channel fit.
- 中 `60-79`: relevant evidence but incomplete, such as AV/integrator/signage media with unclear small-screen or CMS evidence.
- 低 `40-59`: weak adjacent evidence; requires salesperson confirmation.
- 不适合 `<40`: clear exclusion or no relevant evidence.
