# POS Rules

## Product Boundary

The current POS direction is based on salesperson feedback from `2026-06-23`.

The company sells POS hardware only. The main product is a Windows POS all-in-one terminal. Self-ordering kiosk is a customized solution. The company does not provide the customer's POS software or merchant operating system.

Supported peripherals and interfaces may include `MSR`, `iButton`, `NFC`, printer, cash drawer, customer display, monitor, and barcode scanner. These are support signals, not the primary product direction.

Do not treat this POS direction as Android POS or handheld mobile POS by default. The salesperson explicitly said the company does not do handheld mobile POS solutions.

The salesperson's `待确认` values in the POS intake workbook should be treated as confirmed. The notes in the same rows are formal keyword additions or cautions, not unresolved questions.

## High Priority Customers

- POS software and hardware integrators.
- POS agents, distributors, wholesalers, and hardware suppliers.
- POS system vendors with their own cashier software and batch hardware procurement needs.
- Restaurant, retail, convenience store, supermarket, and hotel POS solution providers that deploy cashier hardware for clients.
- Self-ordering kiosk solution providers, distributors, or hardware suppliers only when they may need customized POS/kiosk hardware; this is a low-priority extension, not the core POS all-in-one batch.

High-score evidence should show at least one of:

- POS hardware supply, distribution, agency, wholesale, deployment, installation, or after-sales.
- Own POS/cashier software plus external POS terminal procurement or hardware deployment.
- Restaurant/retail/hotel checkout projects with POS terminals, cashier machines, customer displays, printers, cash drawers, or kiosk hardware.
- Batch procurement or partner/channel language rather than a single end-user purchase.

## Medium Priority Customers

- POS SaaS or cashier software companies with plausible hardware compatibility but no clear hardware supply page.
- Retail/restaurant/hospitality IT integrators with POS deployment or store IT projects, but unclear hardware procurement role.
- Store management, retail management, ordering, or hospitality systems that may need Windows POS terminals.
- Payment or merchant service companies only if they deploy offline POS terminals; otherwise downgrade.

## Low Or Unsuitable Customers

- Ordinary terminal customers such as restaurants, stores, hotels, cafes, supermarkets, and convenience stores.
- Customers needing only one or a few scattered POS devices rather than batch hardware supply.
- Handheld mobile POS focused companies.
- Pure payment gateway, wallet, acquiring, QR payment, or card payment companies with no terminal hardware deployment.
- Pure ecommerce, delivery, marketplace, loyalty, CRM, marketing, accounting, or ERP companies with no POS terminal evidence.
- Competitors/peers unless the user wants competitor observation.

## Target Scenarios

- Restaurant cashier.
- Retail cashier.
- Convenience store and supermarket checkout.
- Hotel front desk cashier.
- Store POS hardware deployment.
- Self-ordering kiosk, self-payment kiosk, and restaurant ordering kiosk when customized hardware is relevant.

- Keywords: `retail technology, restaurant technology, POS integration, store IT, hospitality technology`

P7 Self-ordering / kiosk solution providers:

- Industry: `information technology & services, restaurants, hospitality, retail, computer hardware`

Downgrade or exclude large floor-standing cabinet-only kiosk makers, ticketing/banking/hospital kiosks, ATM, vending machines, and queue-management machines unless there is a clear Windows POS all-in-one, touch ordering terminal, or restaurant/retail POS hardware fit.

## Scoring Levels

- 高 `80-100`: POS hardware distributor/agent/integrator, or POS software company with clear batch hardware deployment/procurement need for Windows POS all-in-one terminals or customized kiosk hardware.
- 中 `60-79`: POS/restaurant/retail/hotel software or IT solution provider with strong cashier scenario but unclear hardware supply or procurement role.
- 低 `40-59`: adjacent retail/restaurant/hospitality system, payment terminal, or store IT evidence but no clear POS hardware fit.
- 不适合 `<40`: terminal end user, pure payment gateway, pure ecommerce/accounting/CRM/marketing, handheld mobile POS focused, competitor, or no POS terminal evidence.

Cut-in examples:

- 询问其 POS/收银软件项目是否需要批量采购第三方 Windows POS 一体机。
- 询问其餐饮/零售/酒店客户部署时是否需要稳定供货的收银硬件、客显、钱箱、打印机或扫码枪配套。

- 先确认其是否只是支付网关或手持移动 POS；若没有线下收银硬件部署，不建议优先开发。
